# Music beds

`sunny-pop.mp3`, `calm-glow.mp3`, `bold-beat.mp3` were synthesised from scratch by `resources/video/music/generate.mjs`
(sine/triangle/saw oscillators, noise percussion, no samples, no third-party recordings or melodies).
