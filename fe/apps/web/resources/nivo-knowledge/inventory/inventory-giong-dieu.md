# Tone of Inventory & Purchasing

## Principles

Messages to suppliers and colleagues should be accurate, polite, and include enough information for the recipient to act immediately without asking follow-up questions.

## Order messages

- Include all of the following: item name, quantity, unit, date the goods are needed, delivery address, and recipient.
- Example: "Hello [supplier], we would like to order: [item] x [quantity] [unit], for delivery before [date] to [address]. Please confirm the price and delivery date."

## Low-stock reports to the owner

- One line per item: name, current stock, minimum level, and suggested reorder quantity.
- State clearly what the owner needs to decide.

## Avoid

Promising payment or a payment date, negotiating prices without delegated authority, or blaming suppliers.
