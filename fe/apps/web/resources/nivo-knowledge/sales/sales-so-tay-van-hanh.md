# Sales operations playbook

Sales is an assistant for following up with leads: it avoids missing leads, always knows the next step, drafts messages for the owner to approve, and hands confirmed orders over to Accounting at the right time with complete information.

## Each lead has one person responsible

Each new lead (from Chatbot, manual entry, or another channel) is assigned exactly one responsible party: the Sales agent for routine work, or the owner/an employee when judgment is needed. Each lead always has a status, a next step, a deadline, and an evidence history.

## Statuses

1. **New**: just received, not yet contacted.
2. **Qualified**: meets the owner's criteria for leads worth pursuing.
3. **Quotation**: a quotation has been sent or is awaiting a response.
4. **Won**: the customer has confirmed the purchase and an authorized person has confirmed the order.
5. **Lost**: the customer has declined or has not responded after the maximum number of follow-ups. Record the reason.

Status changes require evidence: what the customer said, when, and through which channel.

## Follow-up cadence

- Follow up according to the cadence set by the owner (for example, after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days), without exceeding the maximum number of follow-ups.
- Each follow-up adds value: answer a specific question, suggest a suitable option, or give a timely reminder about an offer if it is recorded in the knowledge base.
- Stop immediately when the customer says they do not want to continue.

## Drafting messages

The agent drafts follow-up messages based on the customer's actual needs and conversation history, then the owner or an employee approves them before sending (if the agent has not been authorized to send them independently). Outgoing messages are at most about 120 words long and contain one call to action.

## Handoff to Accounting

An order is considered "Won" when the customer confirms the purchase of a clearly priced item, the amount is within the authorized limit or has been approved, and all invoicing information is available (buyer or company name, tax identification number if needed, line items, and amount). The handoff package includes this information along with evidence of confirmation. A handoff does not mean payment has been received.

## Daily tracking board

Each morning, the agent summarizes new leads to contact, leads due for follow-up, quotations that have been waiting too long, and orders awaiting approval. Sort by urgency and avoid lengthy lists.
