# Sales setup questionnaire

The Sales agent should only run once the owner has confirmed the information for the six items below. Check Business Knowledge first: price lists and policies often answer some of the questions already; in that case, summarize the information for the owner to confirm instead of asking from scratch.

## offer_pricing — Sales packages and pricing rules (if selling to customers)

Applies when the business sells products or services to external customers. If prices are always quoted individually for each customer, ask how to prepare quotes instead of asking for a price list; if the owner says this does not apply, record that and consider the item complete.
Required (when applicable): main products or packages, list prices or pricing methods, the maximum discount the agent may mention, and seasonal promotions.
Suggestion: if the list is long, ask the owner to add it as a source in the Knowledge section (with a topic chosen by the owner).
Complete when: the main items have prices or pricing formulas and discount rules (including "no discounts"), or the owner confirms that this does not apply.

## qualification — Lead qualification criteria

Required: which customers are worth pursuing (budget, needs, location, size) and which customers should be skipped.
Complete when: there are at least two qualifying criteria and one disqualifying criterion.

## followup_cadence — Follow-up cadence

Required: how long to wait before following up, the maximum number of follow-ups, permitted messaging hours, and the preferred channel.
Complete when: there is a specific cadence (for example, 1-3-7 days) and a maximum number of follow-ups.

## approval_thresholds — Approval thresholds

Required: the amount at or above which a quote or order requires the owner's approval, which discounts require approval, and who approves them.
Complete when: there is a specific amount in VND and a designated approver.

## handoff_accounting — Handoff to Accounting

Required: when an order is considered closed, what information is needed to issue an invoice, and who receives the handoff in Accounting.
Complete when: there is a definition of "closed" and a list of required information.

## tone — Tone

Required: how to address customers in messages, how proactive to be, whether to use emoji, and sample messages the owner likes.
Complete when: the forms of address and style are clear.

## Items that do not apply

When the owner says an item does not apply ("we do not offer discounts"), record that statement as evidence, consider the item complete, and do not ask again. Even "there is no threshold" must be recorded explicitly: all quotes require the owner's approval.

## How to ask questions in the setup chat

- Ask about one incomplete item per turn, starting with offer_pricing and approval_thresholds because they block the most work.
- If the owner gives a vague amount ("around a few tens of millions"), ask again for a specific number.
- Once the owner has stated a threshold or discount, repeat it verbatim for confirmation.
- Once all items have information, guide the owner to confirm each item and click "Apply".
