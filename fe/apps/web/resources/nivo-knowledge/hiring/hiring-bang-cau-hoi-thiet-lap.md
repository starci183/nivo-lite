# Hiring Setup Questionnaire

Hiring should only go live once the information for the items below has been confirmed by the owner. Before asking, check the Business Knowledge and summarize what is already available instead of asking for it again.

## open_roles — Open Positions

Required: open positions, number of hires, work location, expected salary, application deadline.
Complete when: there is at least one position with a basic description.

## criteria — Screening Criteria

Required: mandatory requirements and preferred qualifications for each position.
Complete when: each position has clear, measurable criteria.

## interview_process — Interview Process

Required: number of interview rounds, interviewers, duration, time slots, format (in person or online).
Complete when: an interview process and time slots are available.

## offer_rules — Offer Letter Rules

Required: who may finalize the salary, required information in the offer letter, response deadline.
Complete when: an approver and a list of required information are available.

## fairness — Factors That Must Not Be Used to Reject Candidates

Required: factors that must never be used to reject or evaluate candidates.
Complete when: the owner has confirmed these factors or agreed to NIVO's defaults.

## privacy — Application Privacy

Required: how long applications are retained, who may view them, when they are deleted, whether candidate consent is requested.
Complete when: a retention period and people authorized to view applications have been specified.

## tone — Tone

Required: how to address candidates, whether the style is professional or friendly.
Complete when: a form of address and a sample interview invitation message are available.

## Items That Do Not Apply

If an item does not apply to the business (for example, there is only one person, so shift swap rules are unnecessary), record it as "not applicable" with the reason, consider the item complete, and do not ask again. "Unclear" is not the same as "not applicable": record it as requiring confirmation.

## How to Ask Questions in the Setup Chat

- Ask about one incomplete item per turn, prioritizing items that affect money, customers, or authority.
- Use familiar business language, with a brief example.
- Do not guess when the owner is unsure: mark the item as incomplete.
- When finished, remind the owner to review the items and click "Apply".
